Spot the movers in the

Sky

Since 2025, NASA’s SPHEREx mission has mapped the entire sky every six months in 102 bands of near-infrared light. Comets, asteroids, stars, brown dwarfs and even new planets give themselves away by shifting position between images. Enter a patch of sky, flip through the frames like a little video, and find what moves.
